Oral Surgery & Tooth Extraction in Andijan — FAQ

❓Is wisdom tooth removal scary?

Not at all! Dr. Munojat has a remarkably gentle touch and completes the extraction in 10-15 painless minutes.

❓Can I rinse my mouth after extraction?

Do not vigorously rinse within the first 24 hours to protect the vital blood clot.
